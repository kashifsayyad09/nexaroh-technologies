Questions, answered

  

    
What kinds of projects do you take on?
Cloud and DevOps work, custom software, AI and automation, web experiences, security and reliability, and data platforms. If your need sits between these, describe it and we will tell you honestly whether we are a fit.

    
How does a project start?
With a conversation. Use the brief builder or write to tech@nexaroh.com. We then clarify goals, constraints and success measures before proposing a plan.

    
Can you work with our existing systems?
Yes. Many projects extend or modernize what already exists. We begin by understanding your current setup so changes are safe and incremental.

    
How do you approach security?
Security is part of design: least-privilege access, no secrets in code, reviewed changes, and backup and recovery planning. Share any compliance requirements early so we can plan for them.
